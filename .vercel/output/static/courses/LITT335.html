About

This is a 12-week course in which students learn the nuances of editing and develop a thorough understanding of the publishing industry. By the end of the term, you will have mastered the intricacies of wielding the red pen by picking out holes in stories, learning to make every word count, and knowing your semi-colons from your commas. You will learn to differentiate between editing a short story and a news story. As expert editors, you will realize that editing is as much a science of exactitude as it’s an art. To write, you will discover, is to edit. The class will be heavy on workshops. You will learn not just to edit but to edit under pressure. You will also learn what it means to be an editor in the age of AI. Editing is a small component of the publishing journey, which is often a long, drawn-out affair. We will become familiar with this somewhat opaque process and understand the complexities of modern-day publishing. We will learn what happens to a book once it is written, the various roles professionals play to get it published, and why a writer needs an agent. We will cover editorial pitches, book design, and marketing and publicity. How does one find work in publishing? What are some new-age responsibilities of writers in an era of literature festivals and shameless self-promotion? How do some books become bestsellers while others fade into irrelevance? We will address all these questions via in-class lectures, seminars and meetings with professionals.
