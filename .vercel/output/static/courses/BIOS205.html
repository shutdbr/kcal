About

This course is an introduction to the principles that guide the multifarious processes in the cell at the molecular and cellular levels and is intended for a college level audience. The content is an answer to the question: What is the molecular basis of cellular structure and function? Concepts covered include the storage, transfer, and regulation of genetic information, the interactions between molecules, and their assembly and disassembly in cellular functions, the cytoskeleton and its role in cell and tissue shape, molecular traffic between the various cellular locations and across the membrane boundary, signaling within the cell and between cells, cellular metabolism, the cell-cycle, division, growth, development, differentiation and death, and the energy conversions that power all these processes. The course will focus more on principles than on encyclopedic detail, and will be linked to modules taught in earlier trimesters to enable comprehension.
