About

Problem solving activity is an important activity which gives deeper understanding of the theory. Learning theory and solving problems go hand in hand. It gradually enhances the ability of a student to gain deeper understanding of mathematical concepts in different areas and leads the student to the frontiers of research fields. It also helps them to develop skills to write in a logical and comprehensive manner. In the course, problems not only from different international mathematical competitions such as Putnam competition, International Mathematical Competition, International Mathematical Olympiads but also from problem journals such as American Mathematical Monthly and others are used. These are undergraduate competitions as well as plus-two level competitions.
