About

There has been a radical shift in understanding of health and wellbeing. The traditional biomedical approach is giving way to a multi-faceted biopsychosocial approach. Similarly, the processes underlying stress, motivation, wellbeing and team performance in sports demand a greater understanding of the athletes as social beings. Psychology has an indispensable place in the scientific knowledge of health and sports. However, the narrow and individualistic theorisation of different micro and macro-level factors remains a critical challenge to overcome. This course seeks to enable the students to navigate these challenges by focusing on the vibrant psychological processes underlying the fields of health and sports. Taking a lens of groups and identities, we will examine the ‘group’ in the individual that makes these phenomena possible and, at the same time, offers tools for effecting a change. In this course, we shall pursue the questions emerging from the field of health and sports, ground them in scientific knowledge and interrogate them through hands-on exercises.
