About

The course delves into the dynamic processes and mechanisms influencing developmental change, emphasizing the interplay of biological, psychological and environmental factors shaping development from infancy to late adulthood. Additionally, the course provides foundational concepts such as nature vs nurture; continuity vs discontinuity; stability vs instability and the role of critical and sensitive periods in development.
