About

This course is designed to introduce Literature majors to some of the fundamental concepts and practices of literary criticism. We shall begin with a preliminary survey of the history of literary study as a way to propose working definitions of a few key terms: What do we mean by literature? What is a text? Who is an author? And what constitutes literary criticism? This last question will bring us to questions that pertain directly to method: In what ways can a text be read? Does it make sense to speak of a literary method in the same way as we speak, say, of the scientific method? Or, to put it another way, is there anything that all works of literary criticism share in common, or is it futile to look for such a common ground?
