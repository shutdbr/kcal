About

Welcome to an exhilarating journey through modern biology—a discipline that sits at the intersection of every major field of human inquiry. This course will take you on a dynamic exploration of life's interconnectedness and the innovative ways we study it. Beginning with life's origins and evolutionary foundations, we'll traverse scales from molecules to ecosystems, discovering how biology interfaces with various fields ranging from physics and chemistry to psychology and data science. Each module showcases cutting-edge research and real-world applications, such as CRISPR gene editing, AI-powered drug design and pandemic preparedness. Focusing on interdisciplinary thinking, through this course you will see how physics has helped decode photosynthesis at quantum scales, how machine learning revolutionizes protein structure prediction, and how systems biology integrates vast datasets to understand complex diseases. A hands-on laboratory tour midway through the course brings these concepts to life, while modules on global change biology and epidemiology demonstrate biology's critical role in addressing humanity's most pressing challenges. The course culminates in collaborative presentations where you'll envision "Biology X"—the next frontier of biological discovery that excites you most. Whether you're considering biology as a major, exploring it as a complement to another discipline, or simply curious about how life works, this course reveals the extraordinary career possibilities at biology's rapidly expanding frontiers. From biotechnology startups to conservation initiatives, from computational biology to public health—the skills and perspectives you'll gain in biology are increasingly valuable across sectors.
