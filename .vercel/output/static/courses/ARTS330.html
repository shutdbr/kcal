About

A practicum course that offers students with creative ideas and projects to be matched to industry mentors and work on their projects through to execution. Creative ideas that can be tested out in the real world, venture ideas that can be incubated and see initial proof-of-concept, research ideas that go from page to stage, productions that can be staged - all of these constitute the world of creative ideas within the ambit of the course. Specific weeks focus on different phases of creative ideation, and examples from different creative domains are used to illustrate and mentor students on project progress. Students will work on a creative product from idea to execution. Design thinking practices, project management tools and ideation workshops will enable students to work on producing a market-ready product. Emphasis is placed on showcasing the product at relevant public forums and thus act as a bringing together of learnings from several disciplines.
