About

Computation is processing of information. We strive for faster and energy efficient computation. Are there limits? Processing is carried out by physical objects obeying laws of physics. This informs us about possible fundamental limitations to computing. Creation of information costs energy. What is surprising is it cost as much energy to delete information. One approach to minimize this energy cost is to do reversible computations. The conceptual framework to understand this is thermodynamics. Quantum theory adds further possibilities. In this course, these ideas will be presented at a level accessible to undergraduates. This course relies more on research articles than books as the subject is still developing. An exposure to these ideas may help students contribute to the subject.
