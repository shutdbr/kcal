About

Counselling psychology stands out as one of the most popular branches within the field of psychology. This course is designed to introduce students to theories in Counselling. Counselling Psychology offers the ways and means to work with human beings in psychological distress. Thus it is the sub-field that not only offers an application of various understandings of the mind, but also an important purpose to the vast knowledge that the discipline generates. The history of counselling is rich and dynamic. It has been influenced by social, political and intellectual movements at various times. This course aims to familiarise the students with this complex past and present of counselling through some of the major theoretical streams that developed within Psychology.Through thorough exploration and critical analysis of various counselling theories, students will delve into the core principles, concepts, and methodologies integral to the counselling process. Engaging with case studies and role-playing scenarios, students will have the opportunity to contextualise theoretical constructs within real-life counselling contexts. By navigating the complexities of counselling theories, students will gain a deeper understanding of the nuanced approaches employed in the therapeutic encounter.
