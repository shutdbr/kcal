About

The Introduction of Psychology course (PSYC201) introduces students to conceptual building blocks of Psychology, including topics on perception, learning, memory, intelligence, motivation, emotion, and personality. In this way, it levels the field for students from varied educational settings in school who may have a differentiated exposure to psychology as an academic discipline, and prepares them for an undergraduate training in the field. The course seeks to facilitate students in their engagement with the lasting questions around which the discipline has evolved. PSYC201 is also designed to introduce students to some of the classic psychological studies and thereby demonstrate the principles of some of the key areas of modern psychology as a diverse and interdisciplinary science. Students will be expected to read and evaluate classic psychological research studies and critically reflect on the real-life implications of psychological insights.
