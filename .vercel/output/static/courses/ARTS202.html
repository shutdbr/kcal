About

This course will introduce the students to the wide array of possibilities and opportunities offered by the arts as a discipline beyond practice. The field of art writing and criticism is undergoing interesting experimentation in recent times with several critics and writers taking recourse to new creative forms in writing on art. Similarly, the arts are sustained by an interlinked scaffolding of supporters and allies, as can be seen in arts management, grant making, conservation, curation, advocacy, activism, journalism and scholarship. Practitioners in each of these fields, often called mediators or translators of the arts, help in the dissemination, perception, transaction, and value-creation of arts. In other words, they form the ecosystem within which art can thrive. In this course, students will learn how such mediators have engaged with the arts through a series of seminars, talks, and discussions. Through separate writing workshop modules and working groups, students will acquire basic skills in some of these practices. The course aims to instill students with a curiosity to explore future pathways into the arts, encouraging the use of their interdisciplinary skills and knowledge and expanding the horizon of the arts beyond professional practice into everyday life. This course is best augmented with the Performance practicum course offered as well as electives focussed in some of these practices. Learning Outcomes 1. Introduction to various practices of mediation and engagement with the arts. 2. Acquiring some basic understanding and working knowledge of some of these practices, eg. writing, curation, activism etc. 3. Enabling future interdisciplinary pathways into the arts.
