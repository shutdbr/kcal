About

The cell is an integrated device made of several thousand types of proteins molecules, each of which is a molecular machine to carry out very precise tasks. The cell lives in a complex environment and must make decisions about input signals including temperature and osmostic pressure, biological signalling molecules from other cells, beneficial nutrients and harmful chemicals. Information about the internal state of the cell, such as the level of metabolites and cell damage is also important. The information processing functions, that is, responding to these signals, is largely carried out by the transcription factors and networks. In this course we will discuss the engineering principles of biological networks and their implications in cellular functioning and diseases.
