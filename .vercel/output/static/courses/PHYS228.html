About

This course discusses some inherent properties of matter such as surface tension, viscosity, fluid dynamics, elasticity, and various elastic constants. It also discusses the variation of these properties with temperature, pressure, and shape. The basic understanding of these topics is helpful for our day-to-day life as well. The knowledge about the above-mentioned topics can bring openings for research in nonlinear dynamics, soil research, and many interdisciplinary engineering domains, especially if somebody wishes to move into the career path of engineering physics such as civil engineering, fluid engineering, etc. Apart from this, a general knowledge about fluids, beams is helpful in many day to day events of life as well, such as why milk spills easily in a carrying container but not oil. In a nutshell, the students studying this elective course can benefit from having more career paths in the future.
