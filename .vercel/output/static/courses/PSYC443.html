About

This course develops on foundational concepts of social psychology and the research methods courses offered in the Psychology programme at Krea, and introduces students to the exciting world of technological mediations in psychological processes. This is primarily lab-based and will use equipments available in the psychology lab at Krea. We shall introduce students to experimental designs in immersive environments through Virtual Reality MetaQuest3 headsets, the use of EEG and Eye-tracking in studying real-world phenomena from climate change responses to social interactions to mental health interventions. The course aims to be a technologically mediated exploratory journey of social psychological research. It shall be taught by instructors of social psychology and cognitive psychology.
