About

The Introduction to Creative Writing is an intensive writing workshop designed to give students the opportunity to develop their craft via in-class workshops, readings, discussions, and one-on-one tutorials. The discussions will also focus on publishing and the publishing industry. The course will include Zoom sessions with authors and literary agents. The class will mimic the frenzied pace of real-life publishing. There will be deadlines, drafts, and redrafts. We will learn to read like writers and write like writers under pressure. Students will turn in one fully realized piece of fiction (a short story or the first chapter of a novel in progress) and a book review for the course. For the book review, students will choose a work of fiction by an author who comes from a background different from theirs (we will discuss in class what this means) on which to write a paper and give a presentation. The paper will be divided into two sections – the first will be a 1,000-word review of the book, and the second will be an analysis of the reviews the book has garnered in the media. The analysis should include students’ thoughts on the audience the book was written for, the type of marketing that might have gone into the book, the cover, the hooks used to gain publicity, author interviews, etc. You will need to search The New York Times, New Yorker, Guardian, Washington Post, Hindustan Times, Hindu, Financial Times, and other publications for reviews and features on the book. You will give a 10-minute presentation on your review.
