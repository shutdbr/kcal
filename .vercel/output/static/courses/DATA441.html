About

Network science plays a crucial role in modern data science for modeling and analyzing relationships among complex systems. Graphs provide a powerful framework for representing and studying interactions across domains such as social networks, biological systems, brain connectivity, and technological networks. This course offers an applied introduction to graph-theoretic concepts and network science using Python. Students will learn how to represent data as networks and analyze their structural properties, including connectivity, paths, centrality measures, and clustering. The course further introduces network models such as random, small-world, and scale-free networks to understand real-world structures, along with community detection techniques to uncover hidden patterns within networks. Through hands-on exercises and case studies, students will construct and analyze networks, implement key algorithms, and interpret results in real-world contexts. The knowledge gained will support applications in areas such as social network analysis, biological and brain networks, graph-based machine learning, and data-driven decision-making systems.
