About

This course delves into the transformative period known as the cognitive revolution,offering students an in-depth exploration of its profound impact on the field ofpsychology. The cognitive revolution marked a paradigm shift, moving away frombehaviorist approaches and emphasizing the importance of internal mental processes.Students will investigate the theoretical frameworks and methodological advancementsthat define cognitive psychology, focusing on understanding the formal nature ofcognition. Throughout the course, we will trace the historical development of cognitivepsychology, highlighting key figures and landmark studies that catalyzed this scientificshift. Emphasis will be placed on how cognitive psychologists approach the study ofmental processes such as perception, memory, language, problem-solving, anddecision-making. Building on foundational knowledge, this course will explore how thecognitive revolution redefined psychological research, moving it towards a moreinterdisciplinary and scientifically rigorous approach. Students will gain insight into thevarious methodologies employed in cognitive psychology, including experimentaldesigns, computational models, and neuroimaging techniques. By engaging deeply withthese concepts, students will develop a nuanced understanding of how cognitivepsychology addresses complex questions about the mind. They will learn to criticallyevaluate research, appreciate the interplay between theory and empirical findings, andrecognize the ongoing challenges in determining the formal nature of cognition. Throughthis comprehensive exploration, students will be equipped with the skills necessary tocontribute to the dynamic and evolving field of cognitive psychology.
