About

This course is an introduction to the basic notions of Fourier analysis. Three classical topics will be explored: Fourier series, Fourier transforms and the discrete Fourier transform. The basic notions, tools and techniques of Fourier analysis are very important in both pure and applied mathematics (in areas such as signal, image and data processing, etc;) and in physics and computer science. This course is designed to be a gentle, concrete introduction to the subject, with a focus on basic examples and with an eye towards applications. Prerequisites include the basic notions of linear algebra (matrices, vector spaces and linear independence, linear transformations, eigenvalues and vectors and inner product spaces) and single and multivariable calculus. It is hoped that this course will be accessible to 3rd year and 4th year students in mathematics, physics, computer science, and data science.
